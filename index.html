Portal Knight

Cross the gaps. Swing your sword. Reach the portal.

← → Move ↑ Jump Space Sword R Restart level

Three hearts per level. Falling or touching an enemy costs a heart and sends you to its start.

Six levels of hills, steps and valleys. Jump before the edge to reach higher platforms. At a portal, press Enter for the next level.
